## 1. Goal

Elizabeth has a strong interest in **cognitive neuroscience, AI, and music**—especially how people perceive, think, and regulate their emotions.

We will build an original AI product that connects her curiosity about the mind with something she already understands deeply: **sound**. We will build, launch, and distribute this app on iOS store.

---

## 2. Product

### **AI Sound Companion for Emotional Regulation**

### **Vision**

AI sound companion that helps people regulate their emotions in real time through personalized sound.

---

### **Target Audience**

People who experience stress or anxiety in everyday situations and want a fast, simple way to reset.

---

### **Core Need**

When people feel overwhelmed or scattered, they need a quick and effortless way to regain peace and control

---

### **MVP Definition**

We will build a focused experience around **identity-based, contextual sound**:

- The app generates a **personalized soundscape** based on both emotional state and identity and context(e.g. student, athlete, creator, traveler)
- The user listens to quickly reset their state

### **Core Pillars**

1. **Instant Reset:** open → select/describe state → get an immediate tailored soundscape (zero setup).
2. **Identity-Based Sound:** personalization reflects *who you are* (e.g., athlete / coder / traveler), 
3. **Adaptive Calm:** learns what works, when you use it, and patterns over time—becoming a personal regulation engine.
